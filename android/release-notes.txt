- Multi-view: pick each screen's channel from any of your playlists; every screen shows which playlist it uses
- Stream limits per playlist are respected (e.g. "Provider A 1/1"): a playlist with no streams left can't be used for another screen
- Set how many screens each playlist allows in Settings → Playlists (Xtream limits are read from your provider)
- Hold OK on a multi-view screen to change, remove or watch it fullscreen
